💜 Community rules
  1. Be kind. No insults, bullying, slurs, or mocking anyone's story.
  2. No threats, ever. No pressuring anyone, no asking to meet up or move to private chat.
  3. Keep everyone safe: no phone numbers, emails, addresses, links, or social media handles. They are hidden automatically.
  4. What's shared here stays here. Never share someone else's story or private details.
  5. You must be 18 or older.
  6. This is peer support, not therapy or emergency help. In a crisis, call or text 988.
A moderator (with Tosha's oversight) hides messages that break the rules. Breaking them earns a strike: 1 is a warning, 2 pauses posting for 24 hours, 3 removes access. Serious violations remove access right away. Strikes expire after 90 days.

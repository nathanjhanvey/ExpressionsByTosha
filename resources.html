💜 A note from Tosha

I built this page because I know what it feels like to need help and not know where to turn. Every single resource here is free. None of them will judge you. You don't have to be in a full crisis to reach out — feeling alone, scared, overwhelmed, or just like you need someone to talk to is more than enough of a reason to call. I'm proud of you for being here.

Crisis & Suicide Prevention

When you need immediate support

📞
988 Suicide & Crisis Lifeline
24/7 · Free · Confidential

The national crisis lifeline for anyone experiencing suicidal thoughts, emotional distress, or a mental health crisis. Trained counselors answer every call and text. Available in English and Spanish. TTY option available for Deaf/HoH.

Call/Text988
Chat988lifeline.org
Veterans988, then press 1
Nationwide
💬
Crisis Text Line
24/7 · Free · Confidential

If you can't or don't want to call, text is available. Real crisis counselors — not bots — respond to every text. For any crisis: anxiety, depression, trauma, abuse, suicidal thoughts, relationship issues.

TextHOME to 741741
Chatcrisistextline.org
Nationwide
🎖️
Veterans Crisis Line
24/7 · Free · Confidential

Confidential support for Veterans, Service Members, and their families. Staffed by caring, qualified responders — many of whom are Veterans themselves.

Call988, then press 1

Chatveteranscrisisline.net

While You're Healing

Gentle self-care reminders

🌬️
Breathe
4 counts in. Hold for 4. Out for 4. Hold for 4. Repeat. Your body is safe right now.
💧
Hydrate
Drink a glass of water. Stress dehydrates us. This is not a small thing.
🦶
Ground yourself
Name 5 things you can see. 4 you can touch. 3 you can hear. 2 you can smell. 1 you can taste.
🤗
You are enough
You don't have to be okay today. Healing is not linear. Being here is enough.
📞
Reach out
Text someone you trust. Call a friend. Or call 988. Asking for help is brave.
💜
You are not alone
There is an entire community here who understands. You found this place for a reason.
🔍 Finding Local Resources in Your Area

For resources specific to your city or state, these directories connect you with local providers:

• findtreatment.samhsa.gov — SAMHSA's treatment locator by zip code
• 211.org or dial 2-1-1 — connects you with local social services in every state
• domesticshelters.org — find local domestic violence shelters by zip code
• rainn.org/local — find local sexual assault service providers
